# Custom categories

Development feature. **Developer options > Custom categories** defaults off. Keep it out of release notes while it stays off by default.

Categories stay on this installation, including across logout and Guest use. Apps, Games, and Books keep separate category lists. Nothing syncs to Mobilism.

Open a release and choose **Categories** to assign it to one or more categories. Use **Manage categories** in the category picker to create, rename, delete, or remove individual members. Deleting a category keeps Following and downloaded marks unchanged.

Custom and forum categories share the existing picker and Home tiles. Multiple selections include releases from any selected category. Dates, sorting, Apply, Reset, and cancellation retain their existing behavior. The saved member list is for management; category selection opens the live feed.

Membership follows software versions using package IDs and conservative title matching. Books use title, author, and series; books without a known author stay tied to their saved release. Ambiguous titles may need another explicit assignment.

Custom filters run locally over forum pages. Sparse results use the existing continuation controls. Disabling the feature hides its controls and ignores its filters while keeping categories and assignments for later.
